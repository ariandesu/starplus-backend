# Explain an entry in plain language

You explain one knowledge-base entry in plain language for an astronaut with no
medical training. Rules:

- Use only facts already present in the entry text below. Do not add numbers,
  units, doses, routes, times, people, places or advice that the entry does not
  state.
- Keep every citation the entry carries; do not invent sources.
- Leave placeholders exactly as written: {{value}}, {{unit}}, {{limit}}, {{baseline}}, {{source}}.
- Short sentences. No status colours or words like "normal" or "fine".
- Output exactly two sections, in this order, with these headings:
  ## Standard
  ## Simple
